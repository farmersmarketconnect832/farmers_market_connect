About Farmers Market Connect

Farmers Market Connect is a community-based digital platform designed to connect local farmers with customers. Farmers can promote their fresh agricultural products and customers can easily discover locally available products.


Our goal is to support local farmers, reduce unnecessary middlemen and promote fresh, local and sustainable food.
